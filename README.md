# CyberarIA (CYAI)

CyberarIA is an ethical cyber-defense AI project under evaluation. The proposed service would help consenting users locate images of themselves that may have been published without permission. Facial recognition, AI agents and token payments are proposed features, subject to feasibility, privacy and legal review. No live service is announced here.
